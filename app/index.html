Install Hawkeye

Hawkeye runs from your home screen like any other app — no app store needed. It takes about ten seconds.

📱 Android

  1. Open hawkeye.com.ng in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top-right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install to confirm.

Or install it directly:

⬇ Download the Android App

31 MB. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser — that prompt is normal for an app not yet on Play, and it only applies to this one install. The file is served from hawkeye.com.ng itself; do not install a Hawkeye APK from anywhere else.

🍎 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open hawkeye.com.ng in Safari. Chrome and Firefox on iPhone cannot install it — it must be Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, top-right.

The Hawkeye icon then sits on your home screen and opens full-screen. Alerts work once it has been added.

How it works

Three Steps to a Rig-proof Record

No training required. If you can take a photo, you can defend the vote at your polling unit.

1

Witness the Count

Stay for the public counting after voting closes. Photograph the result sheet (EC8A) the moment it's announced.

2

Sign & Submit

Your report is signed on your own phone with GPS and time — proof it came from that place, at that moment.

3

Corroborate

Counts only become trusted when independent observers at the same unit agree. One person can't fake it — that's the point.

Why trust it

Built so nobody — including us — can quietly change a number

Every report is chained to the one before it on a public ledger. Change anything and the chain breaks visibly. Anyone can verify it — no permission needed. Verify the ledger yourself →

  • Tamper-evident ledger anyone can audit
  • Reports signed on the observer's device — keys never leave the phone
  • Independent observers must agree before a count is trusted
  • Phone numbers stored only as one-way hashes
  • Open evidence: photos, GPS and totals visible to everyone

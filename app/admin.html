Organisation codes

Room requests

Every new situation room claims somebody: a campaign names its party, a civil-society room its organisation (picked from the list in app/cso/manifest.json, or typed under Other and shown here as UNLISTED — being listed is not verification). The room admits nobody — no invite link, join or copied roster works — until you approve the claim here. Every party and organisation is checked the same way, and at most one room per party or organisation per race and area can be approved. Rejecting a party keeps the room, without the label; rejecting an organisation keeps the room but it admits nobody new. Rejecting an approved room withdraws its claim. A party room created by a coordinator you named under Organisation codes → Party pre-approval is approved on creation and shows here as auto-approved (withdraw it like any other) — unless it would duplicate an approved room, in which case it waits here. Rooms made before this (no claim at all) are counted and settled with the button below.

Linked accounts

Accounts counted as one voice in every agreement count (results, the photo quorum, docket verdicts). A cluster keeps what its FIRST report said. Nobody is banned or told; honest observers see nothing different. Account ids are masked; no network address or location is stored or shown.

Register Maintenance

    
The INEC register spells some seats more than one way, so a single
      constituency can appear as two. That inflates every count derived from it — the
      Senate board once listed 116 districts for an election with 109, and the coverage
      card sent observers to districts that were duplicates of ones already covered.
      This applies the known corrections from
      backend/src/data/register_name_fixes.json.

    
It was a hand-written curl until now, which meant it did not
      get run. It is idempotent — it matches on the wrong spelling, so a
      second run finds nothing left to change and is harmless.

Close a Declared Race

    
On the night, once INEC's returning officer has announced a result:
      write it into backend/src/data/declarations.json, deploy, then run this.
      Each race it closes drops itself from every follow list and sends the
      people who were following it one alert naming the declared winner and the top three —
      in their notification centre and on their phone.

    
Dry run first. It prints the exact notification each entry
      would send, word for word, and how many people would get it, and writes nothing.
      A push cannot be recalled, so Close is locked until you have seen one.
      Both are idempotent: a race already closed is reported as skipped, never
      announced twice.

About Hawkeye

An independent, nonpartisan record of what was announced at each polling unit — documented, verified and published by ordinary Nigerians.

Why It Exists

Elections are won at the polling unit, then at collation. When numbers can change on the way up, trust collapses. So every report here is photographed, signed on the observer's own phone and chained on a public ledger — and a count is trusted only when independent observers at the same place agree, backed by GPS and matching venue photos.

What we are — and are not

Hawkeye does not declare winners, and its tallies are not official results — those come only from INEC. It is a record built to aid scrutiny, unaffiliated with INEC, any party, candidate or campaign.

Who Runs Hawkeye

Built and operated by IniXien, LLC (© 2026). Observers' reports, and the ledger they form, stay open for anyone to read and verify.

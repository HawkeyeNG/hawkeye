News & updates

What has changed in Hawkeye, newest first.

Election days

16 January 2027: President, Senate and House of Representatives. 6 February 2027: Governorship and State Houses of Assembly.

  1. 9 November 2026

    Photograph a big result poster in parts

    The result poster INEC pastes up at each polling unit (Form EC60E) can be too big for one clear photo. Observers can now take up to three photos of it, top to bottom, and the app joins them into one before it is signed.

  2. 4 November 2026

    177,299 polling units

    INEC's polling-unit directory now lists 453 more units, all in Delta State. Hawkeye's register, maps and unit search carry all 177,299.

  3. 29 October 2026

    Counts read off the sheet photo, on the website too

    The website and Hawkeye Lite now suggest each party's count from the result-sheet photo, with the same reader as the app. The observer still checks every figure before sending.

  4. 23 October 2026

    IniXien Hope Initiative is registered

    The not-for-profit that runs Hawkeye is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission as Incorporated Trustees, IT No. 9958777.

  5. 14 October 2026

    Hawkeye on WhatsApp

    Follow the Hawkeye WhatsApp channel for updates about the app and the 2027 elections.

  6. 11 October 2026

    Each race shows INEC's final list

    Each race now shows the parties on INEC's final list of candidates, so observers see only the parties on their own ballot. See all races.

  7. 10 October 2026

    Find your polling unit offline

    On the website and in Hawkeye Lite, "Near me" now works without a network once your state's units have downloaded.

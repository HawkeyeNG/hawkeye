Keep Hawkeye Independent

Hawkeye is a free, nonpartisan programme of IniXien Hope Initiative, a Nigerian not-for-profit registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission as Incorporated Trustees (IT No. 9958777). It takes no political money and carries no advertising.

  • Partner with us. Civil-society groups, newsrooms and universities can work with us on training observers and publishing polling-unit results.
  • Fund the work. Grants and donations pay for the servers, the phones and the people who keep Hawkeye running.
  • Volunteer. The most useful help of all is free: become an observer at your own polling unit on election day.

Contact

To partner with us or fund the work, write to info@hawkeye.com.ng, or see About & Contact.

Hawkeye will never message you asking for money, passwords or one-time codes.

Privacy & Data

Hawkeye is built to protect the people who use it. This explains exactly what we collect, why, what becomes public, and what stays private.

The English version of this document is the authoritative one. Any translation is provided for convenience; where they differ, the English version applies.

What We Collect
  • Your number — as a hash. One-way, never reversible. We cannot see it, show it, or hand it over; it exists so one number equals one observer.
  • Your reports — the counts, two live photos, and where each was taken.
  • A device fingerprint — hashed, so one phone cannot report the same race twice.
  • Your Telegram link — the chat ID only, so the bot can reach you. We never read your messages or contacts.
What Becomes Public

Your counts, photos and their locations go on a public ledger and cannot be edited or deleted — that permanence is what makes them worth anything. They are tied to an anonymous observer ID, never to you. Incident media appears only after human review.

What Stays Private
  • Your actual number — only the hash exists.
  • Your identity — no report is linked to your name or number.
  • One-time codes — they expire in minutes and are deleted on use.
What We Never Do
  • Sell, rent or share your data.
  • Run ads or third-party trackers.
  • Use tracking cookies — only a sign-in token and your keys, kept on your device.
Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Phone numbers are HMAC-hashed, reports are signed on your own device with a key that never leaves it, and access is rate-limited and bot-screened.

Your Rights

You can delete your account yourself at any time: open your profile and choose Delete my account. This removes your signing key, device link, Telegram link and alert subscriptions. Two things remain, by design: public ledger entries (permanent and anonymous — that permanence is what makes them trustworthy) and the one-way hash of your number, kept so a deleted identity can't be reused to file duplicate reports. Re-registering the same number restores your original observer ID. Other Nigeria Data Protection Act requests: info@hawkeye.com.ng.

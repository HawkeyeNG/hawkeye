## 2\. Compliance guardrails for every ad (read before writing new copy)

**Always:**

* Say **independent / non-partisan** and that **official results remain INEC's**.
* Keep the ask civic: observe, record, verify, practise, report safely.
* Point at a real page on hawkeye.com.ng.

**Never** (each of these risks rejection, or worse, an account strike):

* Name or imply support/opposition for any candidate, party, or their logos.
* Predict, allege or imply that the election will be rigged/stolen. Say "record
what is announced", not "expose the fraud". Fraud allegations can trip civic-
integrity/misinformation policy even when ads are otherwise compliant.
* Claim or imply official, INEC, or government status or endorsement.
* Tell people how to vote, or discourage voting.
* Promise Hawkeye's numbers are the real/official result — they are tentative and
unofficial by design.
* Advertise an Android app download — **the Play listing is not live yet**; point
to the web app instead.

* **Geo:** Nigeria → **Osun State** (add Osogbo, Ile-Ife, Ilesa, Ede, Iwo if
city-level is available). Expect thin volume — Nigerian X skews Lagos/Abuja.
* **Keywords:** Osun, Osun 2026, Osun election, Osogbo, INEC, polling unit,
election observer, party agent, result sheet, EC8A, collation.
* **Interests:** News, politics \& current affairs, Nigerian news, civic \&
social organisations, technology.
* **No** custom/lookalike audiences — not permitted for cause-based ads.

**Pacing:** get certified and **live early**; do not save budget for the final
week. Platforms impose closing restriction windows on election ads, and ads
already delivering impressions are the ones that survive into election day.
Also expect **1.5–3× normal CPMs** in Osun as parties bid up that geo.

Saturday, 16 January 2027

Stay for the count

On Saturday 16 January 2027, vote, then stay at your polling unit until the result is announced, and photograph it with Hawkeye. Your photo becomes part of a public record anyone can check.

Hawkeye is independent and nonpartisan: not INEC, not the government, not a political party.

Three steps before 16 January

  1. Get the app. Hawkeye Election Monitor or Hawkeye Lite, free on Google Play and the App Store. Get the app →
  2. Save your polling unit. The unit where you vote. Do it now, not on the day. Save your unit →
  3. Practise. The free practice run takes a few minutes, needs no sign-up, and nothing you enter is published. Start the practice run →

On election day

Vote, then stay for the count, or come back before counting ends. When the result is posted, photograph it in Hawkeye and submit. Photograph each result sheet: Presidential, Senate and House of Representatives. No signal? Hawkeye saves your report and sends it when you have signal.

Key dates

Saturday, 12 December 2026
National Practice Day. Observers across Nigeria run the same mock election, 08:00–18:00 WAT. Only the combined count is shared, and practice is never counted as a result. About Practice Day →
Saturday, 9 January 2027
National Practice Day. A final run, one week before the vote.
Saturday, 16 January 2027
Election day. Presidential and National Assembly elections. Stay for the count.

Stay safe and follow the law

  • No result is worth your life. If there is trouble, leave, and report it later from somewhere safe.
  • Never interfere with election officials, voters or materials. Follow officials' instructions.
  • Follow the Electoral Act and INEC's rules. Photograph only what is posted or handed out.

Do more

    A ward captain makes sure someone stays for the count at every polling unit in one ward. Read the captain's guide, then apply →

    When two observers at the same unit report the same numbers, its count is verified. Once your unit is saved, open My Profile and tap Invite a Second Observer to send a link set to your unit.

Installing the App

Android asks a few questions because this build does not come from the Play Store. All of it is normal.

  1. Tap Hawkeye.apk in this chat, or find it in your Downloads.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this app — it applies to this one install.
  3. Play Protect will say the app is unknown. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Hawkeye and sign in with your phone number.
Two Hawkeyes on your phone is expected. This test build installs beside the public app — same name, same crest. Neither replaces the other.

Osun Governorship Election
Saturday 15 August

Vote Safely

  • Go early. Vote, then move away from the crowd.
  • Never argue or confront anyone. Step back first — no result is worth your safety.
  • Keep your PVC and phone out of sight in a crowd.
  • If it is safe, stay for the count — your result is announced at your own unit.
And when it is declared, photograph the result sheet. Sent through Hawkeye it becomes a permanent public record — timestamped, and impossible to change afterwards.

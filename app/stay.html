Hawkeye is independent and nonpartisan: not INEC, not the government, not a political party.

Three steps before 16 January

  1. 1

    Install Hawkeye

    Hawkeye Election Monitor or Hawkeye Lite. Free on Google Play and the App Store. →

  2. 2

    Save your polling unit

    The unit where you vote. Do it now, not on the day. →

  3. 3

    Start the practice run

    A few minutes, no sign-up, and nothing you enter is published. →

On election day

  1. Vote
  2. Stay for the count Or come back before counting ends.
  3. When the result is posted, photograph it and submit Photograph each result sheet: Presidential, Senate and House of Representatives.

No signal? Hawkeye saves your report and sends it when you have signal.

No result is worth your life.

Follow electoral officials' instructions. Never interfere, never argue. Photograph only what is posted or handed out.

Key dates

  • Saturday, 12 December 2026 National Practice Day. Observers across Nigeria run the same mock election, 08:00–18:00 WAT. Practice is never counted as a result.
  • Saturday, 9 January 2027 National Practice Day. A final run, one week before the vote.
  • Saturday, 16 January 2027 Election day. Presidential and National Assembly elections. Stay for the count.

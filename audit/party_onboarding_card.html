FOR PARTY POLLING AGENTS

Report your polling unit in five steps

  1. 1Get Hawkeye. Scan the code, or open hawkeye.com.ng/download. Small phone? Get Hawkeye Lite.
  2. 2Sign up free. Got an ORG code from your party? Type it in the code box.
  3. 3Save your polling unit, then tap your party's invite link and Join.
  4. 4Practise on Saturday 12 December. Nothing is published.
  5. 5On election day, stay for the count. Photograph the result sheet (EC8A) and send the counts.

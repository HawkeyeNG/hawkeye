Ward Captain's Guide

A ward captain makes sure someone stays for the count at every polling unit in one ward. You are a volunteer, and Hawkeye stays independent of every party.

What a ward captain does

  1. Fill the uncovered units in your ward. Once you are approved, the Hawkeye team tells you personally which units still need someone.
  2. Get each volunteer through the practice run, then the National Practice Days on Saturday 12 December and Saturday 9 January.
  3. In election week, send reminders: save your polling unit, charge your phone, and stay for the count.
  4. On 16 January, vote, then stay for the count at your own polling unit and photograph the result when it is posted.

How to invite people

  • Your own invite link is in My Profile, under Invite. Share it on WhatsApp or by text. Each person who signs up with it shows on your profile.
  • For groups and notice boards, share the Get Hawkeye page.
  • Each volunteer saves their own polling unit in the app. Never collect a list of who is going where.

Stay nonpartisan

  • Recruit anyone, from any party or none. Party agents are welcome on the same terms as everyone else.
  • No party names, logos, colours or slogans in anything you send about Hawkeye.
  • Say "record what is announced". Never promise to expose rigging, and never predict a winner.
  • Hawkeye is not INEC and not a party. Say so first.
  • Never offer money or gifts for joining or reporting.

Stay safe and follow the law

  • No result is worth your life. If there is trouble, leave, and report it later from somewhere safe.
  • Never interfere with election officials, voters or materials. Follow officials' instructions.
  • Follow the Electoral Act and INEC's rules. Photograph only what is posted or handed out.

What Hawkeye never does

  • It never shares who watches which polling unit — not with you, not with a party, not with anyone.
  • It never publishes coverage below local government level.
  • Being a captain gives you no access to anyone's details in the app.

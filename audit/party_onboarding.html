3Set up your Situation RoomParty leadership

A private dashboard for your party in one race: which polling units your agents have reported, where coverage is thin, and who has yet to file. It runs in a browser on a computer.

  1. 1
    Email Hawkeye your contacts. Write to osaretin@hawkeye.com.ng with: the race and area you will cover; one person to receive your organisation codes; and up to 10 room coordinators. For each coordinator, send the Observer # shown on their My Profile page, or the ORG code they signed up with.
  2. 2
    Sign up (section 2). Then, on a computer, open hawkeye.com.ng/situation-room.html and sign in.
  3. 3
    Create the room. Fill in Campaign or organisation (the room's name), Type: Campaign, Party: your party (required), and Which election (then the state or seat, if asked). Click Create situation room. A room's name may name only its own party.
  4. 4
    Wait for verification. Hawkeye checks every party's room the same way, and approves at most one room per party for each race and area. Until then a banner says the label is being verified, invite links do not work, and you can set the room up meanwhile. You are notified when it is verified. A room created by a coordinator named in step 1 is approved at once.
  5. 5
    Install it on the computer. In Chrome or Edge, click Install in the room's header bar. It opens in its own window and stays signed in.
  6. 6
    Invite your people. Click Invite observers, then Copy the link (hawkeye.com.ng/join/…). Send it in your party's own groups. Each person signs in and taps Join. A link lasts 30 days. Revoke a link that leaks, and Create another link at any time.
  7. 7
    Give access. In the Team tab, find the person and click Give access. Choose a Role and, for a coordinator, the Area, then Confirm. A person must join (step 6) before they can be given access.
  8. 8
    Confirm units. An agent's saved polling unit arrives as a proposal. Click Confirm, or Change unit. An agent can decline a unit, and can always report from where they actually are.

Who can do what in your room

RoleWhoWhat they can do
OwnerWhoever created the roomEverything. The only one who appoints managers, renames or deletes the room.
ManagerAppointed by the ownerThe whole race, and everyone in it. Appoints coordinators.
CoordinatorOne zone, state, local government or wardManages only the people in their area, and sees the whole race's totals. Appoints coordinators below their own level.
MemberAnyone who joined by linkReads the room: their own row and the room's totals. Changes nothing.

Your room is yours alone

  • Other parties and the public never see your roster or your room.
  • No early figures: results reach your room on the same public feed, at the same moment as everyone else.
  • Unit by unit, a room sees only its own agents. Reports from anyone else count in the area totals.

4Organisation codesParty leadership

ORG-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX

An organisation code lets an agent sign up without waiting for a one-time code. The agent types it in step 3 of section 2.

Each code signs up one agent.It works once, and is tied to that phone number for good.
Hawkeye issues them.In batches, as a CSV file, to the one contact your party names.
The same limit for every party.One code per polling unit in your race and area, counted from INEC's register.
A code does not add anyone to your room.Send your invite link as well. Agents tap it and join.
Hand them out one at a time, privately.Never post the list in a group. A leaked code can be withdrawn.
New accounts only.An agent who already has a Hawkeye account needs no code: they sign in and tap your link.

The Codes tab in your room

Shown to the room's owner, or to a coordinator Hawkeye named for your party, once the room is verified.

  1. 1
    Check. Paste codes, or click Upload CSV with the file you were given, then Check. Each code shows Unused Used (the day only) Withdrawn or Not one of yours.
  2. 2
    Withdraw. Tick unused codes, or Select all unused. Click Withdraw selected and confirm. They stop working at once. Accounts already made with your codes are not affected.
  3. 3
    Undo. Click Undo straight after, or Restore on a withdrawn code. Only codes your room withdrew can be restored, and only while your code limit has room.
  4. 4
    Download the results, or Download unused codes only to keep handing them out.

The checker never says who used a code. Only the codes are sent to Hawkeye; the rest of your file stays on your computer.

5Election dayPolling agents

Before the day

  • Sign up and save your polling unit (section 2).
  • Tap your party's invite link and Join. The room then appears in My Groups.
  • Open Ready for election day in the menu. Fix each row until it shows Ready.
  • Practise on 12 December or 9 January: run the practice election (Party A to F). Nothing you enter is published.
  • Update the app before each Practice Day.

On the day

  1. 1
    Arrive at your assigned polling unit. In My Groups, tap I'm at my unit. On the web or Lite, tap it in your party's room.
  2. 2
    Stay for the count until the result is announced and posted.
  3. 3
    Photograph the result sheet (EC8A) and the polling venue, live in the app.
  4. 4
    Check the unit and the election. Enter the counts exactly as written on the sheet.
  5. 5
    Send. No signal? The report waits on the phone and sends itself when signal returns.

HAWKEYEParty onboarding guideChecklist and terms

Stay safe

  • Report after the count, from where you feel safe.
  • Follow officials' instructions. Never argue over a result.
  • No figure is worth anyone's safety.

One agent, one phone, one account

In each election, a phone's reports count for the first account that reports from it. Reports from a second account on that phone are kept but not counted. Each agent should use their own phone.

What your room sees on the day

  • Overview: units reported, how many of your agents have reported, a coverage map, where you are thinnest, and reports as they arrive.
  • Party totals: the same figures the public board shows.
  • Coverage: local government, ward and unit, each marked On their unit, Filed elsewhere or Yet to report.
  • Team: who has checked in, their assigned unit, and where they reported from. A different unit is a discrepancy to explain, not a verdict.

§The same terms for every party

  • Identical terms. Free for every registered party: the same code limit, the same room check, and one approved room per party per race and area. This guide is the same for every party.
  • Independent. Hawkeye is not INEC and not affiliated with any party. It declares no results. Official results are INEC's alone.
  • Never sold. A party's room data is never sold and never shared with another party.
  • Public and anonymous. The counts and the result-sheet photo are published under an anonymous observer ID, never a name or number. The venue photo is not shown publicly.
  • The agent decides. Joining lets the room see the reports an agent files from then on. Agents can leave at any time in My Groups.
  • Codes stay internal. Which party issued a code is never shown publicly and never affects how a result is checked or counted.
